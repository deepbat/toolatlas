# Out-of-box batch — 18 November 2026

142 tools → **148 tools**. Eight added, two removed. No new runtime
dependencies, no server, no uploads — every new tool runs on-device.

## Added

| Tool | Route | Why it is different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land Area Converter (India) | `/tools/land-area-india/` | Bigha means a different area in Punjab (½ acre), UP/Bihar (3,025 sq yd) and West Bengal (14,400 sq ft). No generic converter knows that. Also marla, kanal, guntha, cent, ground, biswa, kattha. |
| India Govt Exam Age Checker | `/tools/exam-age-eligibility/` | UPSC, SSC CGL, IBPS/SBI PO, SSC GD, Railway NTPC and NEET age limits against a cutoff date, with years left. |
| Sunrise, Sunset & Moon Phase | `/tools/sunrise-sunset/` | Solar calculation from latitude/longitude/date plus moon age and illumination. No location lookup or weather API. |
| Aadhaar / PAN / ID Masker | `/tools/id-masker/` | Masks an Aadhaar, PAN, mobile or bank account number before sharing, with a format check that never claims verification. |
| Photo to Colouring Page | `/tools/photo-colouring-page/` | Sobel edge detection turns a photo into a printable black-and-white outline page for kids. |
| Colour Blindness Simulator | `/tools/colour-blind-simulator/` | Machado-matrix simulation of protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and total colour blindness, side by side with the original. |
| Privacy Pixelator | `/tools/photo-pixelator/` | Destroys detail by pixelation (not a reversible blur) so faces and number plates cannot be read. |
| UPI QR Generator | `/tools/upi-qr/` | Builds a standard `upi://pay` link and QR code with optional amount and note. Works with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM. |

The four form-based tools live in `src/lib/extra.ts` (rendered by
`UtilityApp`); the four visual tools live in `src/tools/outbox/App.tsx`
with metadata in `src/lib/outbox.ts`. All eight are `beta` on first
release.

## Removed

- **Image Metadata Viewer and Remover** (`image-metadata`) — a full
  duplicate of Photo EXIF & Metadata Stripper (`remove-exif`), which
  already inspects and strips metadata in batches. Its chain entry now
  points at `remove-exif`.
- **Reverse Text** (`reverse-text`) — a novelty with no real job. The
  text toolkit keeps Word Counter, Case Converter, Text Diff and the
  other working tools.
