# Tool selection and publisher research

Reviewed 12 November 2026. These sources informed feature selection, technical limits and monetization preparation. Tool collections are qualitative demand signals, not independent traffic measurements. No search-volume figures or revenue projections are claimed.

- Our Online Tools, product collection: https://ouronlinetools.com/ . Repeated jobs include upload-limit image compression, PDF assembly, EMI, percentages and text cleanup. Vendor self-description; not audited usage data.
- Toolslay, product collection: https://toolslay.com/ . Broad categories include document/image processing, text, developer utilities and calculators. Used for category coverage, not copied designs or content.
- pdf-lib, primary library documentation: https://pdf-lib.js.org/ . Browser-side PDF creation, copying pages and modifications. Does not promise arbitrary PDF compression or Office conversion.
- MDN Web Docs, platform reference: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SubtleCrypto . Browser cryptography for checksums and hashes; secure-context requirements and algorithm support.
- Google AdSense eligibility, official publisher guidance: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en . Original, useful content, ownership/access and policy compliance matter. No approval guarantee.
- Google AdSense disapproval guidance, official publisher guidance: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/81904?hl=en . Insufficient content, navigation and content quality can prevent approval. A tool count or collection of metadata is not enough on its own.
- GitHub general privacy statement, primary hosting policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Hosting has its own technical request-data practices even when tool inputs are processed locally.

Priority: browser-only utilities that can be genuinely finished and checked. Avoid unsupported AI claims, fake live data, paywalls, forced sign-ups, upload services and large model downloads. Add useful instructions, worked examples and honest limits rather than generating near-identical keyword pages with non-working features.
