# ToolAtlas catalogue

26 curated on-device tools in 4 drawers (Portal & Print, Privacy Shield & Local Files, India Desk, Clever Studio). Processing runs in your browser, subject to browser, codec and device support.

### PDF Editor - `/tools/pdf-editor/`
Edit, annotate, sign and fill PDFs on your device: text, highlights, whiteout, drawing, shapes, images, links and form fields.

### Signature Pad & Transparent PNG - `/tools/signature-maker/`
Draw or clean a scanned signature, remove paper and auto-crop a transparent PNG.

### Bulk File Renamer - `/tools/bulk-renamer/`
Stack filename rules, preview every change and export renamed files in a ZIP.

### PVC Card Maker (Aadhaar, PAN, Ayushman) - `/tools/pvc-card-maker/`
Turn an ID card PDF or photo into print-ready CR80 PVC cards: auto-detect and crop, 300 DPI, A4 sheets of 2, 4 or 10 cards.

### Photo EXIF & Metadata Stripper - `/tools/remove-exif/`
Inspect and remove hidden photo metadata in batches. JPEG/PNG support lossless stripping.

### Land Area Converter (India) - `/tools/land-area-india/`
Convert land area between acres, hectares and Indian units — bigha, marla, kanal, guntha, cent and more.

### India Govt Exam Age Checker - `/tools/exam-age-eligibility/`
Check if your age fits UPSC, SSC, banking, railway and NEET age limits, as of the exam cutoff date.

### Aadhaar / PAN / ID Masker - `/tools/id-masker/`
Mask an Aadhaar, PAN, mobile or bank account number before sharing it — only the last digits stay visible.

### Exact KB - Photo, Signature & PDF - `/tools/image-target-size/`
Fit photos to a KB limit, whiten signature paper and combine marksheets into one size-checked PDF.

### PDF Background and Colour - `/tools/pdf-background/`
Change the page background, make a PDF greyscale or black and white, or adjust brightness and contrast for scans.

### PDF Compare - `/tools/pdf-compare/`
Compare two PDFs by text and by page picture to see what changed.

### PDF Form Filler - `/tools/pdf-form-filler/`
Fill the fields of a fillable PDF form and save it, with an option to flatten the result.

### Passport Photo Maker (India) - `/tools/passport-photo/`
Crop a portrait to India passport 35x45 mm, PAN 25x35 mm or a custom size, and print a sheet of copies.

### Income Tax Calculator (India, FY 2025-26 and 2026-27) - `/tools/income-tax-india/`
Compare new and old regime tax by financial year, age and deductions, with slab breakdown, rebate, surcharge and cess.

### Number to Words (Rupees) - `/tools/number-to-words-inr/`
Write an amount in Indian words with lakh and crore, for cheques and invoices.

### GSTIN, PAN and IFSC Checker - `/tools/gst-pan-validator/`
Check the format of a GSTIN (including its check character), PAN and IFSC offline.

### Privacy Pixelator - `/tools/photo-pixelator/`
Pixelate a photo so faces, number plates and addresses cannot be read, then download the safe copy.

### UPI QR Generator - `/tools/upi-qr/`
Make a QR code that opens a UPI payment for your UPI ID, with an optional amount and note. Works with GPay, PhonePe and Paytm.

### Phone-Scan A4 Prepper - `/tools/doc-flatten-print/`
Turn crooked, shadowy phone photos of IDs or receipts into crisp, whitened 2-up A4 printouts.

### True PDF Redactor - `/tools/pdf-redaction-verify/`
Permanently black out sensitive text and rasterize redacted pages so hidden data cannot be copied.

### Bank Cheque Print Alignment - `/tools/cheque-overlay/`
Format and print payee, date and rupee words aligned to standard CTS-2010 Indian cheque dimensions.

### Screenshot Frame & Redact - `/tools/screenshot-mockup/`
Frame or paste a screenshot, then blur or black out names, numbers and faces before you share it.

### Silent Drain Calculator - `/tools/subscription-burn/`
See the real 5-year and 10-year compounded cost of small recurring subscriptions and daily habits.

### Guest Wi-Fi Printable Card - `/tools/wifi-poster/`
Create a clean tabletop A5 card with a scannable Wi-Fi QR code for your home, office or shop.

### Copy Text from Picture (OCR) - `/tools/image-ocr/`
Read printed text out of a photo or screenshot and copy it, fully on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

### Local File Converter - `/tools/file-converter/`
Convert WebP, PNG and JPG images or turn CSV into JSON and back, all in your browser with no upload.
