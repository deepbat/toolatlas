Office engine licenses and source

This experimental local converter uses ZetaOffice, based on LibreOffice, with the ZetaJS wrapper and Noto Devanagari/Gurmukhi fonts. It is not affiliated with Microsoft or Sejda.

Runtime build source commit: efaf0670b4d055f838a2849becb10f08aa06a257. The engine binary is unmodified. JavaScript glue, font injection and asset splitting are provided separately.

Painter engine (alt/)

The alt/ converter renders DOCX with docx-preview and paints the PDF with pdfkit and fontkit; XLSX is read with SheetJS CE. No document data leaves the device. Fonts: Carlito and Caladea (SIL Open Font License 1.1), Tinos (Apache License 2.0), Noto Sans/Serif/Devanagari/Gurmukhi (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
